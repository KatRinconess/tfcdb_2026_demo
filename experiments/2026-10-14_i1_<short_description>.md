## Procedure

1. Remove vial of CD8+ T cells from liquid nitrogen
2. Thaw vial in 37C water bath for 2 minutes
3. Pipette cells into 15mL conical with 10mL of  pre-warmed RPMI dropwise
4. Spin down 15mL conical AT 400g for 5 minutes
5. Aspirate supernatant 
6. Add 1mL of RPMI and count cells
7. Add appropriate amount of dynabeads
8. Add media to set concentration to 1E6 cells/mL 
9. Plate 1mL of cells in each well of a 24-well plate
10. Place plate in 37C incubator for 48 hours before removing dynabeads
